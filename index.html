When AI Joins the Team:Rethinking Collaboration in Software Engineering

We invite SE researchers and practitioners to examine how AI is transforming collaboration within global software teams.

FSE 2027July 12–16, 2027Shenzhen, China

About the Workshop

Software engineering has historically studied collaboration among humans, but as AI shifts from assisting individual developers to becoming part of the team, we must rethink how it reshapes the social and organizational fabric of teamwork. When AI Joins the Team will bring together software engineering researchers and practitioners to develop a shared vision for AI-enabled collaboration that supports diverse backgrounds, abilities, expertise, and teamwork styles. We will explore emerging forms of human–AI and AI-mediated collaboration, including how AI influences communication, coordination, decision-making, participation, and inclusion within software teams, drawing on perspectives from software engineering, HCI, education, design, and policy. This full-day workshop will combine interactive activities with discussion of future research directions, extending our prior workshops at HCII 2025 and IEEE VL/HCC 2025 toward emerging forms of AI-enabled collaboration.
